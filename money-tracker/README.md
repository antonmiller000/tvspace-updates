# Hamanets

A one-screen money tracker for Android. Two wallets (hryvnia and euro), a total shown in either
currency, Add and Spend buttons, and a history you can delete entries from. The euro rate comes
from the National Bank of Ukraine and can be set by hand.

- `Hamanets.apk` is the installable app (Android 8.0+).
- `app/assets/index.html` is the whole interface; `app/src` is the small Android shell around it.
- `./build.sh` rebuilds the APK (see the comment at the top of the script).
